# Legal and responsible-use notes

This document separates the license for 360GNH code from the rights attached to content processed with the software. It is practical project guidance, not legal advice.

Original 360GNH project code and documentation are offered under the [MIT license](../LICENSE). Third-party packages, fonts, and icons keep the licenses listed in [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

The MIT license does not grant rights to names, logos, provider services, imported videos, transcripts, articles, voices, or other content that is not part of this repository.

## Content you import

You are responsible for having permission, a license, or another lawful basis to copy, transform, translate, publish, or narrate imported material. Public availability is not the same as permission to republish.

The YouTube workflow imports the transcript of one public video and keeps its canonical source URL. It does not download video or audio, process playlists, bypass access controls, or remove attribution. Operators must still comply with applicable law and the current terms of YouTube and the transcript provider they configure.

Do not use the project to process private, paywalled, confidential, personally identifying, or unlawfully obtained material.

## External AI and speech providers

The local deterministic model sends no source content outside the machine. When an operator enables OpenAI or Supadata, the selected source or canonical video URL is sent to that provider as documented in the [security policy](../SECURITY.md). The operator is responsible for reviewing the provider's current terms, privacy commitments, data-location requirements, and account permissions.

AI-generated narration should be disclosed to listeners. Do not imitate a real person without the rights and consent required in the relevant jurisdiction.

## Publishing outputs

Keep source attribution and timestamps when they help readers verify a claim. Before publishing an edited transcript, translation, quiz, study guide, clip list, debate, or audiobook, review it for factual errors, copyright restrictions, privacy, and required disclosures.

If you distribute a compiled web build, include `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and the license texts under `docs/licenses/`.
