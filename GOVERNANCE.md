# Governance

360GNH currently uses a maintainer-led model.

## Maintainer

Nguyễn Duy Hưng is the founding and primary maintainer. The maintainer sets scope, accepts releases, resolves security reports, and has final responsibility for licensing and provenance.

## How decisions are made

- Bug fixes and small compatible improvements proceed through pull-request review.
- New product surfaces, providers, persistence, public hosting, governance changes, and breaking interfaces begin with a public issue or design note.
- Decisions should cite concrete user needs, tests, measurements, or threat analysis. Popularity alone does not override safety or scope.
- Rejected proposals receive a brief reason and may be reconsidered when evidence or project capacity changes.

## Contributor path

Consistent contributors may receive issue triage and review permissions. Commit access is granted only after repeated, high-quality work and demonstrated respect for security and provenance boundaries.

If maintenance can no longer continue, the maintainer may nominate a successor with a public handoff. No contributor is promised a role or benefit merely for participation.
